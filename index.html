The loop

Four steps, in this order. It is a cycle: step IV takes you back to I.

I

Recruit

You open a bag at the tavern and an outlaw comes out. Paid in $BOUNTY: 90% goes to the vault that pays everyone, 10% to the treasury.

II

Rob

Send them to work, up to 10 at once per wallet. On the map they break crates, find chests and dodge traps; the vault pays every epoch, split by weight.

III

Repair

Every epoch of work spends useful life. At zero the outlaw is captured, and low life also lowers the fusion odds. Repairing costs $BOUNTY, which goes back to the vault.

IV

Fuse

Two become one of a higher rank — if the odds allow. The odds are shown before you confirm, always.

…and back to I

Never two alike

Never two alike

visually distinct looks — combining rank, body build, five head types, four eye shapes, four tunics, seven pieces of gear, beard, scarf, skin, hair and the cloth's hue shift. Adding the three stats, which roll with two decimal places, the space passes 6.2 quintillion combinations.

With 10,000 recruits, the chance of two identical ones stays below 0.000001%. Every outlaw is born from a 32-byte seed: the same seed always returns the same outlaw, with the same stats and the same pixels. The traits are derived with sha256 on purpose — it runs exactly the same in Node and inside a Solidity contract, so the chain can prove what the generator produced.

The five terrains

From the road to the castle: more crates, more chests, more traps. Every cleared room moves on to the next terrain, and more Strength clears it faster. The map comes from a seed and is a showcase — what each outlaw earns is decided by the contract. Below, the chests are revealed; in play they stay hidden inside the crates.

What doesn't exist yet, and what can go wrong

  • The contracts are on testnet only, with open code on the explorer and 70 automated tests, but no external audit. The token here is worth nothing.
  • It is not a skill game: the map shows the gang working, but how much each one earns is decided by the contract (the outlaw's weight × the vault).
  • The economy is sustainable in tokens, but real-money value depends on the average player spending more than they cash out. That is game-design work, not tokenomics, and it is where most P2E games fail.
  • A paid bag with a random result is a loot box. The odds are shown before every confirmation by design — but the topic is under regulatory discussion in several countries, including Brazil.
  • Nothing here is a promise of financial return.
  • Play with a test-only wallet. OUTLAWS never asks for your secret phrase, never messages you offering support, and never asks for an "unlimited" token approval. The official address is the one on this page.
